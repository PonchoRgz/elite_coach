### Race Proximity Protocols
- **Pre-Race (2-3 weeks out):** Reduce volume by 20-40%, maintain intensity, prioritize sleep and nutrition. Target positive TSB.
- **Race Week:** Openers 2 days before (short intervals at race pace). Carb-load 48h out. Hydration focus.
- **Post-Race (1-2 weeks):** Active recovery, reduced intensity, assess body composition impact.
